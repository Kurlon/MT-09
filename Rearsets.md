Lots of options for the 24+ MT-09s

- Woodcraft (Gen 3 based kit listed for Gen 4, R9 kit is better but still at most only a 10 to 15mm move.)
- Attack Racing (R9 targetted, may require tweaking)
- Bonamici
- Gilles Tooling
- Sato Racing
- Driven Racing
- Spider Racing

Note: If a given kit lists compatibility with both the Gen 4 MT-09 and earlier bikes... it may be a rearset that was designed on a Gen 3 and they just assumed it's good on the Gen 4 too 'cause the mount spacing is the same. The issue with this, the Gen 3 OEM brackets are shorter than Gen 4s, Gen 4s move the pegs back and up compared to Gen 3 plates. If the aftermarket rearset kit doesn't take that change into account, you may find your new kit doesn't actually move the pegs up and back compared to OEM.
